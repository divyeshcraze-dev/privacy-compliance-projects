# DPDPA vs. GDPR Compliance Checklist


## Individual Rights
Both GDPR and DPDPA use a foundation of individual control, although their terminologies vary. GDPR refers to the individual as a "Data Subject," whereas the DPDPA uses the term "Data Principal."

### Shared Rights

* [x] ✅ **Right to Access:** Obtain confirmation and details of personal data being processed.
* [x] ✅ **Right to Correction:** Request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate personal data.
* [x] ✅ **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data when no longer required.

### Exclusive Rights

* [ ] 📊 **Right to Data Portability (GDPR Exclusive):** Obtain and reuse personal data across different services.
* [ ] 📊 **Right to Object to Processing (GDPR Exclusive):** Stop personal data processing based on legal grounds or direct marketing.
* [ ] 📊 **Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Exclusive):** Limit how personal data is processed under specific conditions.
* [ ] 📊 **Automated Decision-Making & Profiling Safeguards (GDPR Exclusive):** Safeguards against automated decisions without human involvement.
* [ ] 📊 **Right to Grievance Redressal (DPDPA Exclusive):** Access a readily available mechanism to register and resolve privacy grievances.
* [ ] 📊 **Right to Nomination (DPDPA Exclusive):** Nominate another individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.

## Obligations and Accountability

Under the GDPR, the data controller is responsible for complying with Fair Information Practices (FIPs) and demonstrating this compliance to statutory authorities[cite: 1]. Similarly, the DPDPA expects data fiduciaries to ensure statutory compliance, irrespective of any private agreements with the Data Principal regarding their respective duties[cite: 1].

Shared Obligations
* [x] ✅ Processor Contracts: Mandatory execution of valid written contracts between controllers/fiduciaries and data processors.

* [x] ✅ 72-Hour Authority Breach Reporting: Mandatory reporting of data breaches to the supervisory authority within 72 hours.

Exclusive & Differing Obligations
* [ ] 📊 Records of Processing Activities (GDPR Exclusive - Art. 30): Formal obligation to maintain detailed Records of Processing Activities (ROPA).

* [ ] 📊 Privacy by Design & Default (GDPR Exclusive - Art. 25): Statutory mandate to build privacy safeguards directly into technology and organizational architecture.

* [ ] 📊 Sub-processor Consent (GDPR Exclusive - Art. 28): Requirement for processors to obtain explicit controller consent before engaging sub-processors.

* [ ] 📊 Mandatory Individual Breach Notice for All Breaches (DPDPA Exclusive - Sec. 8): Obligation to notify affected individuals for every data breach, regardless of risk level.

* [ ] 📊 Digitized Consent & Grievance Systems (DPDPA Exclusive - Sec. 8): Explicit requirement to provide digitized mechanisms for consent management and grievance redressal.
